Our story

Mental wellness for everyone

MindSpace was built with one belief: that quality mental health support should be accessible to anyone, any time, anywhere.

Our mission

Bridging the gap in mental health care

Millions of people struggle with their mental health every day, often unable to access professional support due to cost, stigma, or availability. MindSpace was created to bridge that gap.

We combine compassionate AI technology with evidence-based mental wellness practices to give everyone access to meaningful support — whether it's 2pm or 2am.

We are not a replacement for professional therapy. We are the companion that supports you in between, helps you build daily habits, and empowers you to understand yourself better.
