Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We've tried to write this in plain language rather than dense legal text. If anything is unclear, reach out and we'll explain it properly.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, date of birth (to confirm you meet our minimum age), and optionally your phone number and what brings you to MindSpace.

As you use the app, we store what you actively give us: mood check-ins (mood level, tags, optional notes), journal entries, and your AI chat conversations — including any photos you choose to share in chat.

Why we collect it

  • To provide the core features you're using — mood tracking, chat history, insights
  • To generate your personalized mood insights and (if you opt in) a weekly summary email
  • To keep the account secure and let you log back in

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your mental health data for advertising.

Third parties involved in providing the service

When you use the AI chat or photo mood-check features, the text and images you send are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This is necessary for that feature to work at all. Google's own terms govern how they process that request; we don't control that separately from what you send them through our app.

If you use email-based features (password reset, weekly insights, verification), our email provider processes your email address and message content to deliver that email.

We don't share your data with any other third party, and we don't sell it.

How we protect it

Being transparent about what's actually in place, rather than making claims we can't back up:

  • Passwords are hashed using Django's industry-standard PBKDF2 algorithm — we never store or can see your actual password
  • Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS in production deployments
  • Your data is only accessible through your authenticated account — other users cannot see your mood entries, journal, or chat history

We are a small, independently-run project. We have not undergone formal compliance certification (e.g. HIPAA, SOC 2), and we won't claim otherwise. If that level of assurance matters for your use case, please factor that in.

Your rights

  • Export: download everything we have on you at any time from Account Settings
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and all associated data, also from Account Settings — this is irreversible
  • Correct: update your profile details any time you're logged in

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed from our database.

Not a substitute for professional care

MindSpace is a wellness companion, not a licensed medical or mental health service. If you're in crisis, please use the resources on our Get Help page or contact your local emergency services.

Questions

Reach out any time via our contact page.
